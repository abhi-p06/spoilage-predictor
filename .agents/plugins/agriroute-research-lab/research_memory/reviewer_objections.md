# Reviewer Objection Register

This register catalogs all critical feedback and objections raised by peer reviewers against the original AgriRoute manuscript ("AgriRoute: A Vision-Based Post-Harvest Risk System"). Every item is assigned an owner, an empirical resolution plan, and a tracking status.

| ID | Reviewer | Core Critique / Objection | Category | Empirical Resolution Plan | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **REV1-01** | Reviewer 1 | BiLSTM sequence model reports an unrealistic AUC of 1.000. Suggests target leakage, synthetic artifact, or trivial label thresholding. | Data Integrity / Leakage | Deconstruct sequence generation script. Verify if synthetic labels were derived via deterministic thresholds. Retrain with noise and report realistic calibrated AUC with 95% CI. | `data-integrity-auditor`, `statistical-methodologist` | IN PROGRESS |
| **REV1-02** | Reviewer 1 | Lack of model comparison baselines. Only MobileNetV2 is evaluated without comparative justification against other contemporary architectures. | Model Benchmarking | Train and evaluate MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB0, and ResNet50 under identical splits and protocols. Report macro F1, AUC, latency, and parameter footprint. | `computer-vision-researcher` | PLANNED |
| **REV1-03** | Reviewer 1 | Absence of confidence intervals, uncertainty quantification, and calibration metrics for spoilage risk predictions. | Statistical Rigor | Compute 95% bootstrap confidence intervals ($B=1000$) for all classification metrics. Evaluate Expected Calibration Error (ECE) and Brier Score. | `statistical-methodologist` | PLANNED |
| **REV2-01** | Reviewer 2 | Research novelty is questionable; seems like a routine application of standard CNN image classification and shortest-path graph search. | Novelty & Positioning | Conduct systematic literature search (2020–2026). Construct state-of-the-art matrix. Reframe novelty around dynamic risk-aware multi-modal routing under perishability constraints. | `literature-reviewer`, `research-director` | IN PROGRESS |
| **REV2-02** | Reviewer 2 | Fusion weights ($\alpha=0.6, \beta=0.4$) for vision and sensor scores appear arbitrary and unjustified. | Methodology Rigor | Perform comprehensive sensitivity analysis sweeping $\alpha \in [0.0, 1.0]$. Evaluate objective cost curves and justify optimal operating point mathematically. | `statistical-methodologist`, `research-director` | PLANNED |
| **REV2-03** | Reviewer 2 | Surface computer vision metrics may lag internal biochemical degradation (e.g. ethylene, pectin breakdown) in thick-skinned fruits. | Biological Domain Validity | Explicitly discuss biological limitations of surface RGB imaging in Discussion. Cite post-harvest physiology literature on visual vs. internal decay latency. | `manuscript-editor`, `literature-reviewer` | PLANNED |
| **REV3-01** | Reviewer 3 | Lack of external dataset validation. The vision model was only tested on splits from the same curated dataset. | External Generalization | Evaluate pre-trained backbones on an independent external fruit dataset (out-of-distribution). Report domain transfer performance drop and discuss vulnerabilities. | `computer-vision-researcher`, `data-integrity-auditor` | PLANNED |
| **REV3-02** | Reviewer 3 | Field evaluation is missing. The paper claims real-world logistics benefits but only presents benchtop/simulated tests ($N=0$ physical fleet trials). | Field Viability & Scoping | Transparently reframe the manuscript as an algorithmic framework and simulation study. Fully disclose $N=0$ live commercial fleet trials and detail a field deployment protocol. | `manuscript-editor`, `adversarial-reviewer` | PLANNED |
| **REV3-03** | Reviewer 3 | Missing component ablations. Unclear whether multi-modal fusion and dynamic rerouting provide measurable economic advantage over static routing. | Experimental Ablation | Design and execute full ablation study comparing Static Baseline, Vision-Only, Sensor-Only, Unweighted Fusion, and Proposed Dynamic AgriRoute across 20 scenarios. | `computer-vision-researcher`, `statistical-methodologist` | PLANNED |
