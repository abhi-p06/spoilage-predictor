Understanding Your Results
Every number on this page is explained below — no black boxes.
🎯
Spoilage Probability
What it means

The overall chance that this produce will be unfit for sale or consumption based on all available data.

How it's calculated

A fusion model (XGBoost) combines the visual freshness score and sensor risk score with your entered storage and harvest details to produce this final number.

📸
CNN Freshness Score (Visual Condition)
What it means

How fresh the produce looks based purely on the uploaded photo. 100% means the surface shows no visible signs of spoilage, bruising, or discoloration.

How it's calculated

A MobileNetV2 deep learning model trained on 13,000 labelled fruit images analyses color, texture, and surface patterns in the photo.

📡
Sensor Risk Score (Gas / Environment Risk)
What it means

The spoilage risk estimated from environmental conditions — specifically ethylene gas and CO₂ levels, humidity, and temperature over the past 24 hours.

How it's calculated

An LSTM neural network reads a 24-hour sequence of sensor readings and detects whether the gas and temperature trend indicates active spoilage.

📅
Days to Spoilage
What it means

The estimated number of days before this batch crosses the spoilage threshold and becomes unfit for market.

How it's calculated

Derived from the fusion model output, adjusted for storage type (cold chain extends shelf life) and days already elapsed since harvest.

⏱️
Days Since Harvest
What it means

How long ago this produce was picked. Every day after harvest, ethylene production increases and shelf life decreases — this directly impacts all model predictions.

How it's calculated

Entered manually by the user on the input form.

❄️
Cold Chain Storage
What it means

Whether the produce was kept refrigerated during storage and transport. Cold chain significantly slows ethylene production and microbial growth.

How it's calculated

Entered manually by the user. A "Yes" reduces predicted spoilage risk and extends estimated shelf life in the fusion model.
